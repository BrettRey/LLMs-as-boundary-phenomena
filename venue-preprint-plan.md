# Venue and preprint update plan

## Decision

Target **Philosophical Psychology** for journal submission, subject to checking the
journal's current instructions immediately before submission.

The fit is the paper's conceptual contribution at the intersection of philosophy,
cognitive science, linguistics, and AI. The journal's stated scope includes original
conceptual work relevant to debates in philosophy and the psychological sciences and
explicitly names cognitive science, neuroscience, linguistics, and computer science
among its readership and contributor communities.

The paper should be presented as an **original article**, not as a commentary. Nefdt
remains the principal philosophical case, but the bearer taxonomy, declaration schema,
and empirical applications make the argument freestanding. Submission no longer waits
for Nefdt's manuscript to be published.

## Philosophical Psychology author guidelines

Current guidance checked 2026-08-15: [Instructions for authors](https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?show=instructions&journalCode=cphp20).

Requirements that matter for this paper:

- The journal accepts original articles and book reviews; submissions are in English
  and use ScholarOne Manuscripts.
- Peer review is double anonymized. A public preprint means anonymity cannot be
  guaranteed, so the journal version should still use an anonymous manuscript file and
  avoid unnecessary self-identifying references.
- Submission is format-free: PDF or LaTeX is acceptable, references may use any
  consistent scholarly style, and a LaTeX submission must include its `.bib` file.
  Taylor & Francis applies its house style after acceptance.
- A typical paper is no more than 10,000 words, inclusive of the abstract, tables,
  references, and figure captions. The submission must include a word count.
- The paper needs an unstructured abstract of 200 words and no more than six keywords.
- The submission package should include author details, affiliations, ORCIDs where
  available, funding information, a disclosure statement, a generative-AI-use
  declaration, a short biographical note, and any applicable data-availability
  statement. Acknowledgments and declarations should not appear in the anonymous
  review manuscript.
- If submitted in LaTeX, the PDF is uploaded as the manuscript and the editable source
  files are supplied as well. The current house-style PDF is therefore usable as a
  preprint artifact, but a later journal package will need an anonymous PDF, source,
  and the required declarations.

The current draft is comfortably below the word limit. Before journal submission,
its abstract should be brought to the journal's 200-word target and its current
acknowledgment should be supplemented or replaced by the journal's required
generative-AI and disclosure statements.
