# Source hook: Sripada & Lewis (2026), the cognitive-convergence position
<!-- SUMMARY: strongest pro-convergence interlocutor; supports the Powell analysis and the convergence-mechanism gap, contests "LLMs break the co-variation" · status: filed 2026-08-14, uncited · updated: 2026-08-14 -->

**Source:** Sripada, C., & Lewis, R. 2026. Cognitive convergence: Deep similarities between
large language models and human cognition. arXiv:2607.26179, v1 (28 Jul 2026), 23 pp. Central
note: `literature/sripada-lewis-2026-cognitive-convergence.notes.md`. PDF, extraction, and a
Mindscape ep. 363 transcript (Sripada presenting the same case informally as "cognitive
cousins") filed alongside it.

**Why this project cares.** This is now the live opposite pole to Nefdt's missing-quadrant
placement: where Nefdt gives LLMs language without cognition, S&L argue five-dimension
convergence (dual-process organization, production-system-like architecture, representational
alignment, prediction-driven learning, shared RL grammar). The paper's diagnosis ~-- disputants
track different clusters under a shared label ~-- should apply to *them* too: their explicit
methodological move ("limited utility in counting behavioral similarities; which mechanisms are
the core processing principles we care about?", §2) is a projection choice, and the
alien-vs-cousin frame is another binary the HPC analysis can dissolve.

**What it supports.** Two anchors in the current draft:

- The Powell paragraph (main.tex:70): S&L supply named machinery for *why* convergent
  properties recur ~-- contravariance (Cao & Yamins 2024: harder tasks narrow the solution
  space) plus their coinage "architectural canalization" (shared production-like architecture
  biases which solutions emerge). Directly citable where the draft says pattern extraction and
  inference "may be convergent."
- The homeostatic-mechanism paragraph (main.tex:72), which concedes "the mechanistic story is
  incomplete": S&L's §5 reviews the convergence evidence (Schrimpf 2021; Mischler 2024
  layerwise-cortical gradient; Kryvosheieva 2026) and offers the two mechanisms above. Caution:
  the Gröger et al. calibration critique the draft already cites (groger2026) targets
  representational-similarity metrics of the same family S&L lean on; don't cite S&L's §5 as
  settling what groger2026 unsettles.

**What it challenges.** Main.tex:90 claims LLMs "break the co-variation," sharing capacity
without processing integration, with vondermalsburg2026 (agreement attraction) as evidence. S&L
argue the integration side converges too: garden paths, similarity-based interference (Lewis &
Vasishth 2005 is Lewis's own model; Ryu & Lewis 2025 shows LLM interference metrics predict
human reading times), serial-position and contiguity effects, conjunctive-search costs. The
tension with vondermalsburg2026's partial-divergence finding is real and worth engaging
directly rather than footnoting. One available refinement: S&L's "mechanism" is computational
organization, not biological substrate, so their evidence splits the draft's mechanism cluster
into two layers (substrate: divergent; computational organization: contested-convergent). That
strengthens the projection-purpose analysis ~-- more projections, more boundary structure ~--
but the two-cluster wording at main.tex:21 and :90 would need adjusting to survive it.
